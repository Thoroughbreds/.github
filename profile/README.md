### What we build

Thoroughbreds AI is bloodstock software for agents, trainers and breeders: the people who buy, sell, race and breed thoroughbreds.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalogue scoring** | Score sale lots with your own ratings, conformation and movement notes, photos and private context. |
| **Shortlists and sharing** | Compare lots, build a shortlist and share it with your team, notes and scores attached. |
| **AI lot and sale intelligence** | Pedigree factors, value ratings, recommendations and summaries of inspection notes. |
| **My Stable** | Horse records with a media vault, lifecycle timeline, ownership, pedigree and DNA in one place. |

Available on the web and as a free iOS app for iPhone and iPad.
