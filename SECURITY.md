# Security policy

Thoroughbreds AI Pty Ltd takes the security of our products and our customers'
data seriously. Thank you for helping us keep them safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't report security issues in public GitHub issues, discussions or
pull requests.**

Email **[support@thoroughbreds.ai](mailto:support@thoroughbreds.ai)** with the
subject line **"Security report"** and include:

- what you found and where (URL, app version or API endpoint)
- steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept
- the impact you think it has
- how to contact you for follow-up

## What to expect

| Step | Our target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledge your report | Within 2 business days |
| Initial assessment and severity | Within 5 business days |
| Updates while we fix it | At least every 10 business days |
| Fix | As quickly as the severity requires |

We'll tell you when the issue is fixed and, with your permission, credit you
for the find. We don't currently run a paid bug bounty.

## Scope

In scope:

- thoroughbreds.ai and app.thoroughbreds.ai
- the Thoroughbreds AI iOS app
- our APIs

Out of scope: denial-of-service testing, social engineering or phishing of our
staff or customers, physical attacks, and findings in third-party services we
use (please report those to the vendor).

## Safe harbour

We won't take legal action against you for security research that:

- is done in good faith and within the scope above
- avoids accessing, changing or keeping other people's data beyond the minimum
  needed to show the issue
- doesn't degrade our services for other users
- gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before anything is made public

If you're unsure whether something is in scope, email us first.
